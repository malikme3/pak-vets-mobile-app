Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Pak Vets (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides this Privacy Policy to explain how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Pak Vets mobile application (“App”).

1. Information We Collect

When you use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information: Name, email, and professional details of veterinarians and clinic staff
  • Animal data: Species, breed, tag ID, owner name, phone number, photos, and descriptions of animals
  • Case data: Chief complaints, diagnoses, treatments, notes, and media (photos, audio recordings) associated with veterinary cases
  • Usage data: Technical data such as device type and app interactions to improve the service

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to:

  • Provide veterinary case management, including AI-assisted diagnosis and treatment suggestions
  • Match animals by image for identification and case association
  • Store and retrieve case records, media, and notes
  • Improve the App and our services

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. We use industry-standard measures to protect data in transit and at rest.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services (e.g., cloud hosting, AI services) to process data. These providers are bound by their own privacy and security commitments.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only as necessary to operate the service, comply with law, or protect rights and safety.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us. Applicable rights depend on your location.

7. Children

The App is intended for use by veterinary professionals. It is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or other reasonable means.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: privacy@pakvets.com (or replace with your contact email).
